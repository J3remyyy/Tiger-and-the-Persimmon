The Tiger and the Dried Persimmon

A cold winter night. A hungry tiger dreams of a cow. The tiger comes down from the mountains into the village. He walks past a house and suddenly hears something. He turns back to the house. The tiger listens.

A sound is coming from the house. Inside are a mother and her baby. The baby is crying and screaming. The tiger listens outside the house. The baby hears its mother say: “Stop crying, or the fox will come to our house!” The baby keeps crying.

The mother says: “Stop crying, or the tiger will come to our house!” The baby keeps crying. The tiger hears the crying from the house. The tiger wonders. He thinks: “The baby isn’t afraid of me?” The tiger is confused. The mother offers something: “Stop crying – here is a dried persimmon (gotgam)!” The baby stops crying at once.

The tiger is shocked. He thinks: “What is this gotgam that makes a baby stop crying?” “The baby isn’t afraid of me… but it is afraid of the gotgam!” “The gotgam must be a terrible monster!” The tiger is terrified.

He flees from the house, runs to the barn and slips inside. In the barn, the tiger cowers in a corner, full of fear. A thief is out that night, planning to steal a cow. He sneaks into the same barn. In the dark barn, he searches for a cow. He feels thick fur. He thinks: “This fur must be the cow!” But it isn’t a cow – it’s the tiger. The thief jumps onto its back. The tiger is startled. He thinks: “The gotgam monster has jumped on me!” The tiger panics.

With the thief on its back, the tiger bursts out of the barn. It races out of the village towards the mountains.

They run all night until the sun rises. The thief is shocked. In the morning light, he sees that he is riding a tiger! Terrified, he clings to the tiger’s fur. The tiger keeps running deeper towards the mountains.

The thief spots a tree. He grabs one of its branches and jumps off the tiger’s back. The tiger notices that the weight is gone. He thinks: “The monster is gone!” The tiger lets out a sigh of relief. And he never, ever came back to the village again.
